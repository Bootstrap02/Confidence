LAGOS, NIGERIA GERMANY

the journey you made possible

Dear —,

Before anything else — happy birthday. I hope today is spent surrounded by people who love you, eating well, and feeling, for once, like the one being taken care of.

But I couldn't let today pass without saying something I don't say enough: what you're doing for me is not a small thing. You're opening a door I could not have opened on my own, and asking for nothing in return. I know it costs you — not just money, but trust, and time, and faith in a future neither of us can fully see yet. I don't take that lightly. Not for one day.

I think about it more than you probably know — that while I'm here in Lagos, building and applying and preparing, there's someone in Germany quietly making room for me before I've even arrived. A "thank you" doesn't really cover that. But it's what I have today, so I'm giving it to you as fully as I know how.

One day, I want you to look at where I've landed and know you had a hand in it. I want to make the kind of use of this chance that makes it worth whatever it's cost you. And when I'm able, I want to become someone else's door — the way you've been mine.

Today, though, it's just your day. So — happy birthday. I'm praying for you: for good health, for peace in your home, and for every quiet kindness you've poured into other people to find its way back to you, multiplied. Long life, plenty of it — long enough to see the full return on everything you've invested. Not just in me. In everyone you've ever shown up for.

With more gratitude than fits on this page, Louis
